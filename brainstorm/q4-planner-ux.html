Design Section 2 — Planner UX
Does this layout work? Review both tabs above (Guided Planning + Quick Plan).

Design notes

  • Guided Planning tab — 5-step wizard, shown at step 2 (Route). Aircraft summary collapses above. Saved airports appear as clickable chips under each field.
  • Quick Plan tab — single scrollable form, aircraft pre-selected, all fields visible. For returning pilots.
  • Recent Plans sidebar — "Reuse this plan" pre-fills all fields with a previous flight. Guest users see an empty sidebar with a prompt to sign in.
  • My Aircraft sidebar — quick summary; links to profile to manage aircraft.
  • Guest notice — friendly, non-blocking. Email prompt deferred to generation step.
  • Step 4 (Fuel) — will show calculated range, endurance, and fuel-to-destination with auto-filled aircraft burn rate.
  • Step 5 (Briefing) — generates the full briefing; triggers PDF download and email prompt.
