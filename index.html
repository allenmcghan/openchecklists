What this is

A library of aircraft profiles and checklists for Junco, the open engine and air data node for Part 103 and experimental aircraft.

An aircraft profile is a TOML file that tells Junco how your aircraft is wired: sensor locations, engine limits, fuel capacity, and unit preferences. One file, hand-editable, no toolchain required.

A checklist is what you read before takeoff and reach for in an emergency. Markdown, printable, written for the aircraft the profile describes.

Neither requires the other. A Junco node works from a hand-written profile; a checklist works printed on a kneeboard card.
