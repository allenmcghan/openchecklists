# ParaPlane PM-2 — Normal operating procedures

Version 0 — draft.

---

| Parameter | Normal | Limit |
|---|---|---|

---

## Takeoff

1. Confirm canopy overhead and inflated symmetrically
2. Advance throttle smoothly to full
3. Maintain directional control with brake inputs
4. Lift off at approximately 22 mph — do not rotate early
5. Climb at full power until at least 200 ft AGL, then reduce to cruise
6. Monitor CHT — on a hot day allow CHT to stabilize before sustained climbs

---

## Cruise

- Cruise RPM approximately 5,500–6,200 (both engines)
- CHT should stabilize 350–400 °F; reduce power if approaching 400 °F in
  climbing turns
- EGT split between L and R should stay under 150 °F; investigate wider
  splits on the ground
- Twin sync: keep both engines within 100 RPM if possible, 200 RPM max
- Monitor fuel endurance — land with at least 1.5 gal remaining

---

## Descent

- Reduce throttle — avoid prolonged idle on two-strokes; brief power
  additions every 30–60 seconds prevent plug fouling and keep CHT from
  dropping too rapidly
- Maintain speed above Vs0 (22 mph) at all times

---

## Approach and landing

1. Plan approach into wind
2. At 100 ft AGL: speed check, obstacle clear, wind direction confirmed
3. Throttle to idle on short final
4. Begin brake flare at 10–15 ft AGL
5. Full brakes to stall at foot level
6. Power OFF after touchdown
7. Collapse canopy immediately — pull rear risers or run to one side

---

## Shutdown

1. Idle both engines for 1 minute — allow CHT to begin falling
2. Kill switch OFF — both engines
3. Confirm both props stopped before approaching
4. Collapse and pack canopy
5. Fuel cap confirmed secure
6. If Junco equipped: review post-flight summary for channel failures or
   transition counts before next flight
