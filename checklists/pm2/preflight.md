# ParaPlane PM-2 — Pre-flight checklist

Version 0 — draft. Not validated against the PM-2 POH. Verify against your
aircraft's documentation before flight. The pilot in command is responsible
for airworthiness.

---

## Before going to the field

- [ ] Weather: wind under 10 mph at surface, gusts under 14 mph, no
      convective activity within 20 nm, ceiling adequate for intended flight
- [ ] NOTAM check for the operating area
- [ ] Density altitude calculated — at or below advisory (default: 3,000 ft)

---

## Site inspection

- [ ] Launch area clear of obstacles, power lines, and bystanders
- [ ] Wind direction identified (sock, streamer, or grass)
- [ ] Wind speed acceptable at surface and estimated aloft
- [ ] Abort path clear in case of aborted launch

---

## Canopy inspection

- [ ] Canopy laid out downwind, cells open, no knots or tangles
- [ ] Leading edge free of debris
- [ ] No visible tears, burns, or porosity damage
- [ ] Brake toggles in hand or properly stowed

---

## Line inspection

- [ ] All A-lines clear from attachment point to canopy — no tangles, no broken lines
- [ ] All B-lines clear — no tangles, no broken lines
- [ ] Brake lines routing confirmed through tabs and toggles
- [ ] Riser attachment at cart confirmed — both sides, positive lock

---

## Cart and harness

- [ ] Harness buckles closed and snug
- [ ] Kill switch lanyard attached and functional — test before starting
- [ ] Throttle moves freely through full range and returns to idle
- [ ] Footrests and seat secure
- [ ] Nothing loose in the cart that could contact the prop

---

## Engine inspection

- [ ] No fuel or oil leaks at cylinders, lines, or fittings — both engines
- [ ] Spark plug caps secure — both cylinders, both engines
- [ ] Prop blades: no cracks, chips, or loose hardware
- [ ] Prop bolts torqued (verify per maintenance schedule)
- [ ] Gearbox oil level confirmed (if applicable)
- [ ] Exhaust springs and mounting secure
- [ ] Air intake clean, no debris or water
- [ ] Carb enrichment / choke moves freely
- [ ] Kill switch OFF

---

## Fuel check

- [ ] Fuel level: minimum **3.0 gal** for any flight (reserve: 1.5 gal)
- [ ] Fuel cap secure, vent unobstructed
- [ ] Fuel is fresh — no phase separation, no water contamination visible

---

## Instrument check (Junco equipped)

- [ ] Node powered, BLE link established on phone
- [ ] Aircraft profile loaded — confirm make and model shown
- [ ] CHT L, CHT R reading ambient — plausible and not pegged at limit
- [ ] EGT L, EGT R reading ambient — plausible
- [ ] RPM reading zero — kill switch OFF confirmed
- [ ] GPS fix acquired on phone
- [ ] Alerts: **NO ACTIVE ALERTS**

---

## Engine start

- [ ] Area behind props clear — announce **CLEAR** before starting
- [ ] Kill switch ON
- [ ] Choke per engine manufacturer recommendation
- [ ] Start engine L — idle until stable
- [ ] Start engine R — idle until stable
- [ ] Choke off — both engines running smoothly at idle

---

## Warm-up

- [ ] CHT rising toward operating range — minimum 2 minutes idle
      (longer in temperatures below 50 °F)
- [ ] Both engines running smoothly — no misfires or stumbles at idle

---

## Run-up

- [ ] Area behind props still clear
- [ ] Advance throttle to approximately 75% — hold for 10 seconds
- [ ] CHT and EGT rising normally, no rough running, no unusual vibration
- [ ] RPM sync: both engines within 200 RPM
- [ ] Throttle back to idle — both engines settle smoothly
- [ ] Endurance: adequate for intended flight shown on display
- [ ] Fuel quantity: plausible reading, no low-fuel alert

---

## Final checks before launch

- [ ] Canopy inflated and kite check satisfactory
- [ ] Brakes in hand
- [ ] Throttle at idle
- [ ] Wind acceptable — surface check
- [ ] Launch area clear
- [ ] Phone secured and visible
